# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Jailbroken iOS users configuring Shadow and troubleshooting jailbreak-detection behavior in individual applications. They need to identify affected apps quickly, then inspect recent detector activity without leaving the familiar Settings workflow.

## Product Purpose

Shadow provides per-app control over jailbreak-detection bypass behavior. Its diagnostics should make detector activity easy to scan globally and investigate per application while preserving deterministic behavior and user privacy.

## Brand Personality

Technical, understated, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid web-dashboard styling, decorative analytics, oversized summary metrics, unfamiliar navigation, and interfaces that obscure raw diagnostic facts behind generated conclusions.

## Design Principles

- Follow native iOS Settings conventions so the interface is immediately understandable.
- Prefer dense, structured facts over decoration.
- Lead from an application summary into focused per-app history.
- Reveal secondary detail on demand without hiding the primary event.
- Keep diagnostics local and user-controlled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels and traits, sufficient contrast, and standard touch targets. Never rely on color alone to communicate status or meaning.
